# A Little Universe Made For Parthiv ♡
### A Personalized, Cinematic, Interactive Birthday Surprise Website

> *"Maybe the universe was waiting for the right moment to bring us together."*  
> — Written with love by **your happiness** for **Parthiv ("babesssss!!!")**

---

## 🌟 About This Experience

This is a bespoke, cinematic birthday surprise website designed as a digital love letter, a handwritten diary, and a magical universe combined. It celebrates the real story of Parthiv and his favorite person:
- Crossing paths many times before the universe aligned.
- Meeting on **Ganpati Visarjan day**, marking the true beginning.
- Developing a twin-soul comfort in just **two weeks** that feels years old.

---

## 🌌 Interactive Features & Structure

1. **Section 1: A Secret Invitation**  
   - Dreamy twilight sky with glowing crescent moon and soft drifting particles.
   - Hidden glowing star in the sky: reveals an intimate secret message when clicked.
   - Cinematic entry button: *"Come inside, birthday boy ♡"* with sound chime and ambient soundtrack start.

2. **Section 2: Happy Birthday, My Babesssss!!!**  
   - Handcrafted 2-tier romantic birthday cake in rose gold with interactive candles.
   - Tap cake to trigger rose-gold confetti and light up birthday shimmer.
   - Smooth navigation into the story chapters.

3. **Section 3: Our Story, Written Somewhere in the Stars**  
   - Interactive 3-chapter timeline with bespoke visual cards:
     - **Chapter 1:** *All Those Almost-Moments* (Vintage film frame & orbits brushing)
     - **Chapter 2:** *The Day Everything Felt Different* (Ganpati Visarjan sunset water reflection)
     - **Chapter 3:** *Two Weeks, and You Already Feel Familiar* (Twin stars connecting into a heart)
   - Concludes with the cosmic quote.

4. **Section 4: Things I Love About Our Little World**  
   - 6 scrapbook diary cards with washi tape accents and delicate icons:
     - *The Comfort*, *Our Vibe*, *The Timing*, *Our Twin-Soul Energy*, *Our Two Weeks*, *The Little Things Ahead*.
   - Tap to open and cherish each personal reflection.

5. **Section 5: Open These Whenever You Want**  
   - 3 realistic envelopes with 3D wax seals in Blush Pink, Warm Ivory, and Romantic Burgundy:
     - *"Open when you miss me."*
     - *"Open when you need comfort."*
     - *"Open when you wonder how we happened."*
   - Smooth flap-opening sound and handwritten letter overlays.

6. **Section 6: A Secret Surprise for My Babesssss (The Clue & Star Trail)**  
   - Hidden until unlocked!
   - **Step 1:** The Clue — *"Babesssss, there is one more little thing hidden here for you..."*
   - **Step 2:** The Star Trail — Click 5 sequentially appearing stars in the night sky to reveal progressive story lines.
   - **Step 3:** The Final Reveal — Stars connect into a heart constellation, revealing a sealed digital love letter *"Only for Parthiv ♡"*.
   - Unfolds into the long, intimate birthday love letter with floating stardust.

7. **Section 7: Make a Birthday Wish**  
   - Full-screen scene with a glowing candle flame in soft golden light.
   - *"Send Your Wish Into the Universe ✨"* sends a shooting star into the cosmos with sparkle bursts.
   - Replayable anytime.

8. **Section 8: Babesssss, Pick Your Gifts! 🎁 (Virtual Romantic Boutique)**  
   - Dreamy interactive boutique with blush-pink atmosphere, burgundy accents, and golden ribbons.
   - **Progress Tracker:** Displays opened gifts in real time (*"X of 6 little surprises discovered ♡"*).
   - **6 Interactive Gifts:**
     1. *The Love Letter 💌:* Envelope opening animation revealing a sweet personal message.
     2. *Our Little Universe ✨:* Transparent glass dome with glowing stars and connecting constellations.
     3. *A Virtual Hug 🧸:* Interactive plush teddy bear that bounces and opens its arms with floating hearts.
     4. *Our Firsts ♡:* Two glowing lights gently drifting together to honor our first hug and first kiss.
     5. *Your Birthday Wish 🌙:* Interactive candle with shooting star wish dispatch.
     6. *A Mystery Gift 🎀:* Playful wobbling gift box with golden ribbon burst and unlimited moments.
   - **Final Surprise Reveal:** Unlocks upon opening all 6 gifts — revealing a special sealed letter: *"One last thing from your happiness ♡"*.

9. **Section 9: The Last Little Love Letter**  
   - Warm ivory paper texture, delicate floral corner illustrations, and heartfelt closing letter.
   - Final statement: *"In a universe full of people, I am glad our paths finally found their way to each other."*
   - *"Return to Our Little Universe ♡"* smooth scroll button.

10. **Sound & Audio Design**  
    - Pure **Web Audio API** synthesized ambient romantic chords and music box chimes.
    - Zero external audio dependencies required.
    - Sound effects for star clicks, envelope opening, and wish sending.
    - Floating controls to toggle music, mute sound, or toggle drifting rose petals.

## 🚀 Running Locally

```bash

cd /path/to/babes

# 2. Install dependencies
npm install

# 3. Start development server
npm run dev

# 4. Open in your browser
http://localhost:5173
```

```bash

npm run preview
```

## ✏️ How to Customize Content

All content is centralized in **`src/contentConfig.ts`**!

### Editing Any Text, Messages, or Quotes:
Edit any string directly in `src/contentConfig.ts`. The changes will instantly reflect everywhere across the site, including:
- Parthiv's nicknames and sender moniker
- The Ganpati Visarjan story and timeline chapters
- The 6 diary cards
- The 3 interactive "Open When" envelopes
- The secret star-trail surprise and final love letter

## ☁️ Deployment Guide

### Deploying to Vercel (Recommended — Free & Instant)
1. Push this project to a GitHub repository.
2. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and click **"Add New Project"**.
3. Import your repository.
4. Framework Preset will automatically detect **Vite**.
5. Click **"Deploy"**.
6. Share the generated `.vercel.app` link with Parthiv!

### Deploying to Netlify
1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com) and click **"Add new site" -> "Import an existing project"**.
2. Select your GitHub repository.
3. Build command: `npm run build`
4. Publish directory: `dist`
5. Click **"Deploy site"**.
